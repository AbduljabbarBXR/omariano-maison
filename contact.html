Questions

Before you ask.

A few things people ask most often. If your question is not here, call the house — the answer is usually quicker than an email.

How do I order a book?

Add titles to your bag and send the list to the house over WhatsApp. We will confirm availability, delivery cost and payment before anything is dispatched. Orders are handled by the Omariano team, not by an automated checkout.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes. Delivery is available countrywide through courier, and complimentary within Nairobi on orders over KES 5,000. Rates outside Nairobi depend on weight and destination — call or message for a quote.

What if a title says “out of stock”?

The catalogue reflects the shelves, not a warehouse. If a book is out of stock, call us and we will tell you whether it is on order, suggest an alternative, or reserve the next copy for you.

Can I pay with M-Pesa?

Yes. M-Pesa is the usual method, and card payment can be arranged in the shop. Payment details are shared by the team when an order is confirmed.

Do you supply schools, mosques and libraries?

We do. For class sets, reading lists and institutional orders, write to info@omariano.com with the titles, quantities and date you need them. We will confirm availability and a quotation.

Can you hold a book for me?

Yes, for a short period. Call the house with the title and your name and we will keep it behind the counter. We will confirm how long we can hold it when you call.
